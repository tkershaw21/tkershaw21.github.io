About 7.2 Earth days

About Ganymede

Ganymede is wider than Mercury, making it the largest moon in the solar system. Its surface contains darker, older regions and lighter areas marked by grooves. These features preserve evidence of impacts and changes within its icy crust.

Observations provide evidence for an ocean beneath the surface. Ganymede also produces its own magnetic field. Studying it helps scientists understand how large icy moons develop layered interiors and interact with giant planets.

Interesting fact

Ganymede is the only moon known to have its own internally generated magnetic field.
