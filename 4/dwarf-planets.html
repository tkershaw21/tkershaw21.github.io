Choose a dwarf planet

Dwarf planets orbit the Sun and are rounded by their own gravity, but they have not cleared their orbital neighborhoods.

Pluto

A distant world with mountains, glaciers, and a bright heart-shaped region.

Ceres

The largest object in the main asteroid belt, with unusual bright salt deposits.

Eris

A Pluto-sized world that follows a long, stretched orbit far from the Sun.

Haumea

A fast-spinning, elongated dwarf planet with two moons and a ring.

These are four selected dwarf planets. Makemake is another officially named dwarf planet outside this collection.
