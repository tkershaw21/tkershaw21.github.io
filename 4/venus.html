About Venus

Venus is similar to Earth in size, but its surface environment is very different. A dense carbon dioxide atmosphere traps heat, while clouds of sulfuric acid hide the ground from view.

Its surface has volcanic landscapes and crushing atmospheric pressure. Venus rotates slowly and in the opposite direction to most planets. It demonstrates how strongly an atmosphere can influence a planet's conditions.

Interesting fact

Venus takes about 243 Earth days to rotate once, longer than its approximately 225-day year.
