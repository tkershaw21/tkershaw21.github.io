Haumea

A fast-spinning world

At a glance

Type
Dwarf planet
Size
About 1,740 km equatorial diameter; elongated
Location
Average of 6.5 billion km from the Sun
Year length
About 285 Earth years

About Haumea

Haumea is an elongated dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt. It rotates roughly once every four hours, making it unusually fast-spinning for a large solar system object. Its shape means that its width depends on the direction measured.

Haumea has two moons, Hi'iaka and Namaka, and a ring. Astronomers can investigate distant objects like Haumea when they pass in front of background stars. Changes in starlight reveal details about their outlines.

Interesting fact

Haumea was the first known Kuiper Belt object found to have a ring.
