Eris

A distant icy world

At a glance

Type
Dwarf planet
Size
About 2,400 km in diameter
Location
Average of about 10 billion km from the Sun
Year length
About 557 Earth years

About Eris

Eris is similar in size to Pluto but generally travels much farther from the Sun. It follows an elongated orbit, receiving only faint sunlight in its distant environment. One trip around the Sun takes centuries.

Its discovery helped renew the discussion of how planets should be classified. Eris has a small moon called Dysnomia. Observing the moon's motion helps astronomers investigate the mass of the system.

Interesting fact

Sunlight takes more than nine hours to reach Eris at its average distance from the Sun.
