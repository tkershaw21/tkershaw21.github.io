Europa

An ocean beneath the ice?

At a glance

Type
Natural satellite of Jupiter
Size
About 3,100 km in diameter
Location
About 671,000 km from Jupiter
Orbital period
About 3.5 Earth days

About Europa

Europa has a bright ice-covered surface marked by long cracks and darker bands. Scientists have strong evidence for a salty ocean beneath its outer ice shell. Gravitational interactions help flex and heat its interior.

Researchers study Europa to understand whether its environment could support life. They need to investigate the availability of water, useful chemicals, and energy. No life has been discovered on Europa.

Interesting fact

Europa's possible ocean could contain more water than all of Earth's surface oceans combined.

Source: NASA Europa Facts
