Arrokoth

Two pieces of ancient history

At a glance

Type
Kuiper Belt object
Size
Approximately 35 by 20 by 10 km
Location
About 6.5 billion km from the Sun
Year length
About 293 Earth years

About Arrokoth

Arrokoth is a small, cold object in the Kuiper Belt. It consists of two connected lobes, a structure called a contact binary. Scientists interpret this shape as evidence that two objects joined gently.

New Horizons flew past Arrokoth on January 1, 2019. Its distant location means that the Sun has provided relatively little heating. It therefore preserves valuable clues about the early outer solar system.

Interesting fact

Arrokoth's larger lobe is flattened, so its actual shape is more complicated than a simple snowman.
