About Vesta

Vesta is one of the largest objects in the asteroid belt. Early heating allowed its interior to separate into a crust, mantle, and core. This makes it more complex than many smaller asteroids.

Dawn studied Vesta from 2011 to 2012 before continuing to Ceres. Large impact basins show where collisions removed material. Some meteorites found on Earth are linked to Vesta, allowing scientists to study pieces of its history in laboratories.

Interesting fact

Vesta's Rheasilvia impact basin is about 500 km wide, almost as wide as Vesta itself.
