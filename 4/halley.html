Halley's Comet

A returning visitor

At a glance

Type
Periodic comet
Size
Nucleus approximately 15 by 8 km
Location
Orbit stretches from inside Venus's orbit to beyond Neptune
Orbital period
Approximately 76 Earth years

About Halley's Comet

Halley's Comet has a dark nucleus containing ice, dust, and rocky material. As the comet approaches the Sun, warming releases gas and dust that form a coma and tails. The visible cloud is much larger than the solid nucleus.

Edmond Halley recognized that several historical comet sightings matched a repeating orbit. The comet was later named in his honor. An international group of spacecraft studied it during its 1986 visit.

Interesting fact

Material left behind by Halley produces the Eta Aquarid and Orionid meteor showers.

Source: NASA 1P/Halley
