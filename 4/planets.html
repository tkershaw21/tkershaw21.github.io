Choose a planet

These four terrestrial planets have solid, rocky surfaces. Their atmospheres, temperatures, and landscapes vary greatly.

Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun.

A year lasts just 88 Earth days.

Venus

An Earth-sized planet hidden beneath thick clouds.

Its atmosphere traps enough heat to make it the hottest planet.

Earth

Our home world, with liquid oceans and abundant life.

Earth is the largest of the four terrestrial planets.

Mars

A cold desert world with volcanoes, canyons, and polar ice.

Its landscapes preserve evidence of ancient water.

There are eight planets in our solar system. This category focuses on the four inner, rocky planets.
