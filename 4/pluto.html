About Pluto

Pluto is a dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt. New Horizons revealed mountains, plains, and flowing ice during its 2015 flyby. Its bright heart-shaped region includes a large basin filled with nitrogen ice.

Pluto follows an orbit that is more tilted and stretched than those of the major planets. Its distance from the Sun changes significantly during its long year. Different surface regions show evidence of geological change.

Interesting fact

Pluto and its largest moon, Charon, orbit a shared center of mass located outside Pluto.

Source: NASA Pluto Facts
