Asteroid Eros

A rocky world up close

At a glance

Type
Near-Earth asteroid
Size
Approximately 34 by 11 by 11 km
Location
Orbits the Sun and crosses Mars's orbit
Rotation period
About 5.27 hours

About Eros

Eros is an elongated, rocky asteroid in the Amor group of near-Earth objects. Its irregular outline and cratered surface differ from the round appearance of larger planets. Its orbit does not quite reach Earth's orbit.

NEAR Shoemaker entered orbit around Eros in 2000 and landed in 2001. The mission examined its composition, shape, and surface features, giving scientists a detailed view of a small rocky world.

Interesting fact

Eros was the first asteroid to be orbited by a spacecraft and the first to receive a spacecraft landing.
