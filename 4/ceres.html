About Ceres

Ceres is the largest object in the main asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter. Its gravity has pulled it into a nearly round shape, unlike the irregular shapes of many smaller asteroids.

The Dawn spacecraft studied bright deposits in its craters. These deposits contain salts and provide clues about the movement of briny water. Ceres demonstrates that objects in the asteroid belt can have complex geological histories.

Interesting fact

Ceres was discovered in 1801, making it the first object discovered in the asteroid belt.
