About Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest terrestrial planet. Liquid water covers about 71 percent of its surface. Its atmosphere and active geology help shape the environments in which life exists.

Earth's tilted axis causes its seasons. As the planet travels around the Sun, each hemisphere receives different amounts of sunlight. Earth also has one natural satellite: the Moon.

Interesting fact

One astronomical unit, or AU, is approximately the average distance between Earth and the Sun.
