About Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun. Its surface contains impact craters, broad plains, and cliffs formed as its interior cooled and contracted.

Mercury has a very thin exosphere that provides little insulation. Its sunlit surface becomes extremely hot, while the night side becomes very cold. Despite its location, Mercury is not the hottest planet; Venus is.

Interesting fact

A sunrise-to-sunrise day on Mercury lasts about 176 Earth days, twice the length of its year.
