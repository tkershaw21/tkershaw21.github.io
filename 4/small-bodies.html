Choose a small body

Small bodies preserve clues about the early solar system. Some are rocky, while others contain large amounts of ice.

Halley's Comet

An icy visitor that returns to the inner solar system approximately every 76 years.

Asteroid Eros

An elongated near-Earth asteroid explored by the NEAR Shoemaker spacecraft.

Asteroid Vesta

A large asteroid with a layered interior and enormous impact basins.

Arrokoth

A distant Kuiper Belt object formed from two connected lobes.
