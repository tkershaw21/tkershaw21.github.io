Titan

A moon with weather

At a glance

Type
Natural satellite of Saturn
Size
About 5,150 km in diameter
Location
About 1.2 million km from Saturn
Orbital period
About 16 Earth days

About Titan

Titan is Saturn's largest moon. Its dense, nitrogen-rich atmosphere contains an orange haze that hides much of the surface from ordinary visible-light observations. Radar and infrared instruments reveal dunes, channels, lakes, and seas.

Titan is cold enough for methane and ethane to exist as liquids at its surface. These materials participate in a cycle involving clouds, rain, and flowing liquid. Water ice forms much of the solid landscape.

Interesting fact

Titan is the only moon in our solar system with a thick atmosphere.

Source: NASA Titan Facts
