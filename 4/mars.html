About Mars

Mars is a cold, rocky world with a thin atmosphere. Iron minerals give its surface a rusty red appearance. It has polar ice caps, deep canyons, large volcanoes, and two small moons called Phobos and Deimos.

Dry channels and other landforms show that liquid water once changed the landscape. Rovers study its rocks to understand these past environments. A Martian solar day, called a sol, lasts about 24.6 hours.

Interesting fact

Olympus Mons on Mars is the largest volcano in the solar system.
