Choose a moon

Moons are natural satellites. They can have craters, icy crusts, thick atmospheres, and even hidden oceans.

Moon

Earth's only natural satellite.

Its craters and dark plains preserve billions of years of history.

Europa

A bright, icy moon of Jupiter.

Scientists have strong evidence for an ocean beneath its surface.

Titan

Saturn's largest moon has a thick atmosphere.

Liquid methane and ethane fill some of its lakes and seas.

Ganymede

The largest moon in the solar system.

This moon of Jupiter is wider than the planet Mercury.

Moon distances in this guide refer to the planet they orbit.
