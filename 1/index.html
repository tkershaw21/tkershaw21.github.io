Kiyotaka Ayanokoji

Kiyotaka Ayanokoji is the main character of the anime and light novel series Classroom of the Elite. He is a quiet and intelligent student who attends Advanced Nurturing High School and often keeps his true abilities hidden from the people around him.

Why I Like Ayanokoji

  • I like Ayanokoji because he is extremely intelligent and thinks carefully before making decisions.
  • I admire how calm he stays when he is placed in difficult or stressful situations.
  • I like that he does not always need attention or recognition from other people.
