How Exploration Works

  1. Choose a category: planets, moons, dwarf planets, or small solar system bodies.
  2. Choose an object: select one of four objects in that category to display its information.

Your selections update the Explore page directly. Each object includes an image, description, size, location or distance, and an interesting fact. You can choose another object or return to the categories without opening a different page.

Visit the Learn page to discover the ideas connecting these different worlds.

What Can You Explore?

  • Planets: discover worlds that orbit the Sun and compare their environments.
  • Moons: explore natural satellites and their varied landscapes.
  • Dwarf planets: learn about smaller, nearly round worlds that orbit the Sun.
  • Small solar system bodies: investigate asteroids and comets for clues about the solar system's past.
